**Make New Booking:**

**Questions to ask the Client:**
- What details are required to make a booking?
- Should users need an account to book?
-**Can users book:**
    - Immediate trips?
    - Future dates?
- Should pricing be shown before confirmation?
- Can users edit details before finalising?

**Conversation Transcript**

**Developer:** Rosie
**Customer:** Alexis

**Developer:** What details are required to make a booking?

**Customer:** We are going to need to store a name, email, phone number, dates, 
       billing information, flight numbers, final destination, hotel booking reference (if 
       applicable)

**Developer:** Should users need an account to book?

**Customer:** Yes, they should, because we want to make it easier for them to book another for 
                   example if they are a regular traveller on a certain route. 

**Developer:** Would you want incentives to make an account, e.g. a loyalty scheme?

**Customer:** Yes, so that they book with us more often to maximise profits. It will also let them 
       get better discounts the more they book.

**Developer:** Can users book immediate trips, far in advance, or both?

**Customer:** They need to book at least two weeks before their intended travel date, else they 
       won’t have the chance to get their deposit back if they cancel.

**Developer:** Should it show the final total on the selection screen for hotels?

**Customer:** Yes – all of the related, automatic fees should be shown on the main page with 
       the hotels to maximise transparency.

**Developer:** Should users be able to easily edit the booking before confirmation?

**Customer:** Yes, but they should have to go through each step to make sure it is all correct, 
       and the pre-payment bill should be shown after editing.


**User Story:**

As a registered customer, we want to be able to book a trip by entering our details at least 2 weeks in advance, so we're able to secure transport in time for the trip while also being able to receive the relevant refunds if we cancel due to unseen circumstances. 

**Amend Booking:**

**Questions to ask the Client:**
- What can be changed?
- Is there a time limit for amendments?
- Does changing a booking affect the price?
- Should users pay extra if the new booking costs more?
- Can users amend multiple times?

**Conversation Transcript**

**Developer:** Rosie
**Customer:** Oliver

**Developer:** After booking, what can be amended, after payment?

**Customer:** They can change vehicle requirements and passenger numbers, but not their own details or the taxi company used. They can also change the final destination, with a potential additional fee.

**Developer:** Also with dates/times?

**Customer:** As long as there is a week’s notice

**Developer:** Can users’ amend multiple times?

**Customer:** No more than 1 amendment per 24hrs before week’s notice

**Developer:** If a user was confused, how would they get more information on finding the best 
        amendment to make?

**Customer:** There should be a ChatBot to help the user.


**User Story:**
As a customer, we want to amend specific booking details on our booking within the one week notice period, so that we can accurately update our trip if there are sudden changes.
